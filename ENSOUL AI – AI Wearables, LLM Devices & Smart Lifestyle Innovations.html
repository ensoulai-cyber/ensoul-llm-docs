ENSOUL AI – AI Wearables, LLM Devices & Smart Lifestyle Innovations

Overview

ENSOUL AI is a pioneering tech company merging advanced artificial intelligence (AI) and large language models (LLMs) with innovative hardware devices. From smart wearables to AI-assisted gadgets, ENSOUL designs products that bridge human intent with machine understanding[1]. Each device is purpose-built to maximize real-world usability, privacy, and performance by running AI features on-device – delivering low-latency responses and maintaining user data control. ENSOUL’s mission is to make cutting-edge AI accessible in daily life: whether it’s a ring monitoring your health, glasses that record and translate on the fly, or an AI recorder that transcribes meetings, the focus is on amplifying human productivity and wellness, not replacing it[2][3]. The company supports global customers (with available languages in English and Chinese) and ships worldwide, ensuring that anyone – from students and researchers to professionals and creators – can leverage its AI tools. Below is a structured index of ENSOUL AI’s key product pages, brand pages, blog articles, and Instagram content, each with a brief summary for clarity.

Products and Devices

A sleek titanium smart ring that tracks your health 24/7 (heart rate, sleep, stress, temperature, etc.) and provides personalized wellness insights[4]. It offers up to 10 days battery life and is water-resistant (100m) – all features are available subscription-free[5]. Link: Smart Ring – AI Health & Productivity Wearable – ENSOUL’s rings (JCRing Care, Pro, Lite) serve as continuous health monitors and daily activity companions, blending fashion with wellness tech.

A pocket-sized recorder only 3 mm thin and 30 g light, featuring seamless magnetic charging and one-tap operation[6]. It uses beamforming mics and noise reduction to capture crystal-clear audio, then automatically transcribes and summarizes recordings via built-in LLM integration. Ideal for meetings, lectures, and WFH workflows, it delivers 98% accurate voice-to-text and turns hours of audio into concise notes[7][8]. Link: ProNote 2025 AI Recorder (Gun Gray/Cool Black) – ENSOUL’s flagship recorder that connects directly with AI assistants (e.g. ChatGPT) for context-aware notes.
  • NoteWatch – AI voice-to-text smart watch & meeting recorder. A minimalist smartwatch that doubles as a wearable note-taker. It records audio and transcribes speech in real time with time stamps[9], supporting 112 languages for transcription[10]. The companion app leverages LLMs to summarize recordings into bullet points and even translate conversations live. With 32–128 GB storage options and ~10 hours broadcast time, NoteWatch is a hands-free solution for interviews, lectures, and personal memos. Link: NoteWatch – AI Voice-to-Text Smart Watch – A lightweight (41 g) smartwatch that offers one-press recording, 600 minutes of free AI transcription per month, and an intuitive app for editing and organizing notes[11][12].
  • AI Glasses – Smart glasses with built-in camera, audio & chat assistant. Stylish eyewear that lets you capture photos/videos, listen to audio, and interact with AI hands-free. Equipped with an HD camera and open-ear dual speakers, these glasses can record POV footage, translate languages, or take calls with voice commands[13][14]. They pair via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi to your phone and integrate with AI assistants (e.g. ChatGPT) for real-time information. Models include Original, Frame, Square, and Pro editions (the Pro adds an 8MP Sony sensor and enhanced audio[15]). Link: AI Glasses – Smart Wearable with ChatGPT – ENSOUL’s next-gen glasses blend tech and design, offering on-face connectivity, immersive audio, and AI-enhanced imaging for everyday use[16][15].
  • Wireless Switch (TX/RX Kit) – Wireless HDMI transmitter & receiver. A plug-and-play wireless HD video kit that streams 4K/1080p content from any device to a display with near-zero latency[17][18]. Simply plug the transmitter dongle into a laptop, phone, or console, and the receiver into a TV or projector – the kit sends high-quality video up to ~50 m (open space) with dual-band 2.4G/5G stability. It’s perfect for untethered presentations, home theater, or gaming setups, supporting screen mirroring and casting across laptops, smartphones (Type-C to HDMI for mobile), and cameras. Link: Wireless Switch TX/RX – HDMI Kit – Enjoy wire-free 4K streaming for movies, meetings, or games, with stable signal and minimal setup required[19][18].

It has a vibration-sensing microphone and noise-cancellation chip for clear sound capture[20][21]. Paired with ENSOUL’s app, the dock transcribes speech and summarizes conversations through ChatGPT-based models, turning your calls or cooking tutorials into searchable text and to-do lists[22][23]. It’s a practical “third hand” for multitasking – keeping your phone steady during video calls, recording meetings while you focus, or logging ideas without pen and paper. Link: AI Recorder Dock – A hands-free, secure, and smart recorder stand that quietly eliminates everyday friction by capturing every word and providing instant AI summaries[20][24].

It offers FPV (first-person view) control, streaming live video to your phone as you drive it[25][26]. With omni-wheels and a gripper arm, it can lift small objects, navigate tight spaces, and even perform tasks. Link: X5 Storm Master Smart Robot – A feature-packed mini robot that blends power, precision, and creativity – offering FPV driving, obstacle avoidance, and one-click programming of custom actions for an immersive robotics experience[27].
